Hi ! 

I am a product manager with a track record in engineering, management, and business roles.

I started my career in semi-conductor, building tools to support semiconductor manufacturing in critical situations, requiring zero downtime.

I pursued my career as a DevOps engineer and then a network engineer, scouting bottlenecks and security vulnerabilities in an IT network management platform (SD-WAN, virtual network, SDN/NFV). I built automation pipelines that integrate DevSecOps and cloud-native environments (Docker, Kubernetes, CI/CD) to double the testing capacity.

Next, as a technical product manager, I built a cloud-native platform for telecommunications with the R&D team. I owned the product lifecycle from discovery to post-launch KPIs. That was how I learned product management, through hands-on experience, learning from an external community of experts, and constantly asking for peer-to-peer feedback.

In 2024, I joined Paris 2024 to spearhead an operations team and deliver telecommunications infrastructure that connect the 2024 Summer Olympics to the whole world – 5 billion of audience. (Hints on the competition venues I worked on: 🧗‍♂️, 🚵, 🏃).

Currently, I own the product & business of a new API business unit, building ops and business of fraud prevention family APIs for enterprise customers.

Always happy to connect or swap notes, I'm available on [LinkedIn](https://linkedin.com/in/rahendarosmedi) !
