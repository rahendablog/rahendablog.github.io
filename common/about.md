Hi ! 

I am a product manager with track record spanning engineering, product, management, and sales.

I started my career in semi-conductor, building tools to support semiconductor manufacturing in critical situations.

I pursued my career as a DevOps engineer, and then a network engineer, scouting bottlenecks and security threates in the product delivery chain, to build automation pipelines that solve them.

Next, as first product manager, I built a cloud-native platform for internal clients in telecommunications. From R&D and product discovery to product post-launch KPIs, I learned product management by getting my hands dirty, relying on books, and expert communities.

In 2024, I joined Paris 2024 to deliver telecommunications infrastructure that connect the 2024 Summer Olympics to the whole world – 5 billion of audience. (Hints on the competition venues I worked on: 🧗‍♂️, 🚵, 🏃).

Currently, I work as first business development manager, handling ops and business of API for enterprise customers.
